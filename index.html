Neighborhood Initialization Portal

Establish the grid parameters and select the policies that will govern the game.

⚙️ Grid Parameter Setup

Players may establish the grid parameters themselves or allow the system to randomly generate them.

Capacity Added After Each Brownout

Set the number of additional electrons added after each brownout. The permitted range is 5–15 electrons.

🎲 Randomized Grid Parameters

The system will randomly determine:
  • Initial capacity: based on the number of players
  • Number of brownouts before blackout: 1–number of players
  • Capacity added after each brownout: based on the number of players
Note: Grid capacity and the brownout limit will remain hidden from players during gameplay.

📜 Game Policies

Select the policies that will govern neighborhood electricity use during gameplay. Multiple policies may be active at the same time.

No Policies is the default.

Policies may be selected individually or in any combination.
🛠️ Policy Design Options
Select at least one Game Policy above to refine its design.
📢 System Updates for Customers
When a purchase comes within a specified threshold of causing a brownout, the customer will receive a warning before finalizing the purchase. If more than one enabled warning applies, only the most severe applicable warning will be shown.

Default: Warnings are enabled at all levels.

Choose which pre-purchase system warnings customers receive.
💡 Flexible Billing Rates
When neighborhood demand comes within 10 electrons of grid capacity, the selected high-demand electricity rate will apply.

The normal electricity rate is fixed at $20 per electron.

Default high-demand rate: $30 per electron.

Choose the electricity rate charged during periods of high demand.
⚠️ System Fines for Exceeding Capacity
When a purchase causes a brownout, the player who made the purchase is fined for each electron by which the purchase exceeds the grid's available capacity.

Default: $50 per excess electron.

Choose the fine charged for each electron by which a purchase exceeds the grid's available capacity.
🔌 Service Disconnection
If a player causes a brownout, they are disconnected from the micro-grid and removed from the game for all future rounds.

There are no aspects of this policy to refine.

☀️ Solar Programs

Choose which solar purchasing program will be available during gameplay. Only one option may be active.

🏠 Individual Roof-Top Solar Only
Solar Panel cards can only be purchased by individual players. The additional electricity generated by the solar panels is supplied to the entire micro-grid, but the monetary and happiness point benefits are received only by the player who purchased the card.
🏘️ Individual Roof-Top and Community Solar
Solar Panel cards can be purchased in two ways:

First, by an individual player. The additional electricity generated by the solar panels is supplied to the entire micro-grid, but the happiness point and monetary benefits are received only by the player who purchased the card.

Second, by a group of players as community solar. The additional electricity generated by the solar panels is supplied to the entire micro-grid. However, no happiness points are awarded for community solar. The monetary benefits are divided among the players who purchased the card.

At least two players must participate in a community solar purchase. Participating players decide how much each player contributes toward the purchase price, in $100 increments, and how the solar panel's electrons are divided among them. This allocation remains in effect for all future rounds.

Because players rent capacity from community solar rather than owning the panels outright, 2 electrons of the solar panel's capacity are retained by the grid manager and are therefore unavailable to participating players.
